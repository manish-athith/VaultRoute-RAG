# Enterprise Sales Process

An enterprise opportunity moves through discovery, qualification, solution review, commercial review,
and signature. Sales records the customer problem, decision process, stakeholders, timeline, and known
security requirements in the CRM. Forecast stages reflect customer evidence rather than seller hope.

Security or product commitments must be confirmed by the responsible team before they enter a proposal.
Sales may not promise unapproved features or legal terms. Discounts follow the pricing guideline and
need the stated approval level. After signature, the account owner provides implementation with the
agreed scope, success measures, risks, and key dates. Customer credentials and confidential documents
must remain in approved systems rather than email attachments or personal notes.
