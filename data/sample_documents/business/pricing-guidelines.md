# Pricing and Discount Guidelines

Quotes start from the current approved price book. Standard annual terms may be offered without an
exception. Discounts are based on contract length, committed volume, reference value, and strategic
fit; they are not granted only because a prospect asks near quarter end.

The Sales Officer may approve discounts up to five percent. The Business Manager may approve discounts
up to twelve percent. Larger reductions, unusual payment terms, or non-standard liability language
require CEO and finance review. Every exception records the reason, approver, expiry date, and expected
value in the CRM. A previously approved exception does not automatically apply to a renewal. Pricing
information is confidential and should be shared internally only with people working on the account or
responsible for commercial policy.
