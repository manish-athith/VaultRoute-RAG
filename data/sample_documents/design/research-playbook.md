# User Research Playbook

Research begins with a written question and a decision the team expects to make. The plan identifies
participants, method, consent, data handling, and the people who will review findings. Recruitment
should reflect relevant user groups without collecting information that is not needed.

Participants must understand recording and note-taking before a session starts. Raw recordings and
contact details stay in approved restricted storage. Reports replace names with participant codes and
separate observed evidence from interpretation. A single interview is not presented as a general trend.
Researchers connect findings to supporting sessions, note uncertainty, and record conflicting evidence.
Product teams receive a concise summary and recommended next experiments instead of unrestricted raw
participant data.
