# Board Strategy 2027

ACME will focus on secure enterprise knowledge products during 2027. The founding team has approved
three priorities: reach repeatable product-market fit, improve gross margin, and build a trusted
partner channel. Expansion into a new region requires a board review of legal, support, and data
residency risks before a public commitment is made.

The operating target is sustainable growth rather than growth at any cost. Leadership will review
monthly recurring revenue, retention, gross margin, and cash runway each month. Hiring plans may be
accelerated only when the base forecast preserves at least eighteen months of runway. Acquisition
discussions, financing terms, board votes, and named investor information are restricted to the
founding department. Department plans should use the approved strategy but must not repeat sensitive
financing details.
