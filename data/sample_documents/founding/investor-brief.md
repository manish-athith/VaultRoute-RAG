# Investor Brief

The investor update is prepared after the monthly finance close. It summarizes recurring revenue,
customer concentration, product delivery, hiring, and major risks. Preliminary figures must be marked
as estimates and must never be copied into customer-facing material.

The CEO owns the final narrative. The Founder confirms financing and ownership statements, while the
CTO confirms technical delivery claims. Questions received from investors are recorded in the board
workspace so later answers remain consistent. This brief is confidential and limited to the founding
team. If another department must contribute, it should receive a narrow question instead of the full
brief. Superseded briefs remain archived with their reporting date because the newest approved version
is the source of truth.
