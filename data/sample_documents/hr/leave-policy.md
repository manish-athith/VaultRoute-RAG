# Employee Leave Policy

Employees request planned leave through the HR system and notify their manager as early as practical.
Managers should respond consistently while considering project coverage. Medical details are private
and must not be placed in general team channels or ordinary project documents.

HR maintains the official leave balance and resolves policy questions. Emergency leave may be reported
after the absence begins when prior notice is impossible. A manager may arrange coverage but cannot ask
for unnecessary medical information. Records are retained according to local legal requirements and
are available only to authorized HR staff and the affected employee where applicable. When this guide
conflicts with local law or an approved employment agreement, HR must review the case before a final
decision is communicated.
