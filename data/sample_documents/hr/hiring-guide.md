# Hiring and Interview Guide

Every open role starts with an approved job description, compensation range, interview panel, and
decision owner. Interviewers evaluate the same role-related criteria and record evidence shortly after
each conversation. Protected characteristics and unrelated personal information must never influence a
hiring decision.

HR coordinates candidate communication and access to interview notes. Panel members may view only the
candidates assigned to them. Reference checks occur only with candidate consent. Offers require the
approved compensation band and required leadership review. Rejected candidates receive a respectful
closure message, and their data follows the retention period in the privacy policy. Hiring metrics are
reported in aggregate so individual candidate information is not disclosed outside the hiring team.
